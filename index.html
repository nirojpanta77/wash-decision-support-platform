ABOUT

Nepal Flood WASH Decision Support Platform

Purpose

Identify municipalities with the greatest WASH impact and remaining need during the emergency-to-recovery transition, supporting cluster prioritisation, verification and recovery planning.

Geographic unit

Municipality-level WASH severity analysis informed by ward-level exposure, displacement and response evidence.

Core model

P1 Impact & disruption 45% · P2 Current WASH conditions 35% · P3 Vulnerability & aggravating factors 20%.

Interpretation safeguards

Response footprint and evidence confidence remain separate from Need Severity. P2 is provisional and deliberately conservative where current-condition evidence is sparse.
